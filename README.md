# Pigeon

**Fast, private mail for Gmail on your Mac.** All your Gmail accounts in one calm inbox. Your mail goes straight between your Mac and Google: no Pigeon server, no account, no tracking.

**Website:** https://tahirjaved1.github.io/pigeon/

## Install

```sh
brew install --cask tahirjaved1/pigeon/pigeon
```

Or download **[Pigeon.dmg](https://github.com/tahirjaved1/pigeon/releases/latest/download/Pigeon.dmg)**, open it and drag Pigeon to Applications.

Pigeon isn't signed by Apple yet. The first time you open the DMG version, macOS says it can't verify the developer:
1. Click **Done**.
2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

You only do this once, because Pigeon updates itself. Homebrew handles this step for you.

macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

## Features
- A unified inbox for all your Gmail accounts, with conversations that show clearly who wrote what
- One-click unsubscribe, Quiet sender, a Screener for new senders, Snooze, and an Inbox clean-up you can undo
- Undo Send, scheduled sending, templates and signatures
- Optional AI that writes in your own tone, and **Ask Pigeon**, which answers questions about your mailbox. It runs locally with Ollama, or uses Claude with your own key after asking your permission.
- Phishing warnings, remote images and trackers blocked by default, and a Touch ID lock
- A calendar beside your mail
