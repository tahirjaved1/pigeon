Fast, private mail
for Gmail on your Mac

All your Gmail accounts in one calm inbox. Your mail goes straight between your Mac and Google — no Pigeon server, no account, no tracking.

Download for Mac Install with Homebrew

Version 0.6.0 · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · free

Install

With Homebrew (updates included):

brew install --cask tahirjaved1/pigeon/pigeon

Or download the DMG, open it and drag Pigeon to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the developer (Pigeon isn't signed by Apple yet): click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once; Pigeon updates itself after that.

One inbox, every account

A unified inbox with each account in its own colour, real-time new mail and conversations that show who wrote what.

Quiet the noise

One-click unsubscribe, Quiet sender, a Screener for new senders, Snooze and an Inbox clean-up you can undo.

Writes like you

Optional AI learns your tone from your sent mail and drafts emails and replies the way you write them.

Ask your mailbox

"How many emails haven't I answered this week?" Pigeon searches your real mail, counts, and answers.

Safer by default

Phishing and look-alike warnings, trackers and remote images blocked until you allow them, Touch ID lock.

Calendar beside your mail

Your calendars next to your inbox, invitations you can answer from the email, and events found in emails.
