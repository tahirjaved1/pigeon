The private, AI-powered Gmail app for Mac. Smarter than Apple Mail, more private than Spark, and free, unlike Superhuman.

Download for Mac Install with Homebrew

Version 0.8.0 · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · free

Private by design

No Pigeon server, no Pigeon account, no tracking. Your sign-in stays in your Mac's Keychain.

AI that works for you

Writes in your own tone and answers questions about your mailbox. It runs on your Mac, with no subscription.

A calmer inbox

Unsubscribe, Quiet sender, Screener, clean-up with undo and phishing warnings, all in one free app.

Where your mail goes

Many mail apps route your account through their own cloud to power features like AI, push or send-later. Pigeon has no cloud: it talks to Gmail directly from your Mac.

Want AI? Pigeon's assistant runs on your Mac, with Apple Intelligence or Ollama. If you choose Claude instead, Pigeon asks first and sends only the email you're working on, using your own key.

One inbox, every account

A unified inbox with each account in its own colour, real-time new mail and conversations that show who wrote what.

Quiet the noise

One-click unsubscribe, Quiet sender, a Screener for new senders, Snooze and an Inbox clean-up you can undo.

Writes like you

AI learns your tone from your sent mail and drafts emails and replies the way you write them.

Ask your mailbox

"How many emails haven't I answered this week?" Pigeon searches your real mail, counts, and answers.

Safer by default

Phishing and look-alike warnings, trackers and remote images blocked until you allow them, Touch ID lock.

AI with no setup

On Macs with Apple Intelligence, AI help runs on Apple's own model inside macOS. Nothing to install, nothing leaves your Mac.

Translate any email

Emails in other languages get one-click translation, or are translated automatically. Keep it on your Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Privacy report

Every week, see how many trackers Pigeon blocked and who tried to track you, counted on your Mac only.

Calendar beside your mail

Your calendars next to your inbox, invitations you can answer from the email, and events found in emails.

Install

With Homebrew (updates included):

brew install --cask tahirjaved1/pigeon/pigeon

Or download the DMG, open it and drag Pigeon to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the developer, because Pigeon isn't signed by Apple yet. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once; Pigeon updates itself after that.
