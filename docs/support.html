Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub and include your macOS version and Pigeon version (Pigeon → About Pigeon). Issues are public, so never paste anything private from your mail.

Installing

With Homebrew: brew install --cask tahirjaved1/pigeon/pigeon. Or download Pigeon.dmg. The first time, macOS asks you to allow it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

After an update, macOS may ask once whether Pigeon may use its saved sign-in in your Keychain: choose Always Allow.

Signing in

Sign in with Google opens Google's sign-in page in your browser; approve there and Pigeon opens your mail. If your organisation blocks third-party apps, ask your administrator, or use an app password: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords.

AI help

Turn it on in Settings → AI help. For AI that stays on your Mac, install Ollama and run ollama pull llama3.2 in Terminal. For Claude you need an API key from console.anthropic.com.

Start at login

In Pigeon's Settings, tick “Open Pigeon at login”. It's off until you turn it on. You can also manage it in System Settings → General → Login Items.

Something went wrong

Pigeon keeps an error log on your Mac. Send it along with your question: open Finder, press ⌘⇧G and go to ~/Library/Logs/Pigeon-crash.log.

Remove Pigeon's access

Remove the account in Pigeon's Settings, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
